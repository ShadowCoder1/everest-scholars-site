Questions.

Is it a real professor?

Yes. A professor at a university or research institute, who agrees in writing to mentor the student’s project. If we can’t arrange that within 12 weeks of the first session, you get the full $1,200 back.

In a lab or online?

Either. Some professors work with students in their lab, and others mentor online. We tell you which before the first meeting.

Who runs the journal?

Everest Futures LLC. The Everest Scholars Journal is our in-house journal, and professors and subject experts review every paper.

Do you write any of the research?

Never. Every word of the paper is the student’s own.

Does this guarantee admission or awards?

No. Admissions offices, judges and editors decide those. If the student enters a competition, report our help where the forms ask: Regeneron STS asks about paid mentorship and publishing, ISEF’s Form 2A asks about paid support, and JSHS asks for a statement of outside help.

Who pays, and how?

A parent or guardian, in one payment before the work starts. The 30-minute call is free.
