Paths

Choose your focus. Build beyond it.

Start with one path or combine all three. We build a personalized roadmap around your interests, experience and goals, then pair you with mentors who help you execute it.

01Competitions

Develop a competition-ready research project.

Build, refine and present original work for science fairs and research competitions.

You’ll work on

  • Research strategy and project planning
  • Competition selection and timelines
  • Abstract, paper, poster and presentation
  • ISEF forms and documentation
  • Mock judging with experienced competitors
  • Technical Q&A and presentation coaching

Built for students targeting

ISEF and affiliated fairs · JSHS · Regeneron STS · Stockholm Junior Water Prize · Brain Bee · other research competitions

02Research & Publication

Turn an idea into serious research.

Develop a project, learn how to approach researchers, and work toward a manuscript or other appropriate research output.

You’ll work on

  • Finding a focused research question
  • Literature review and study design
  • Finding relevant professors and labs
  • Personalized professor outreach
  • Research execution with mentor guidance
  • Data analysis and interpretation
  • Manuscript development
  • Preprint, conference and journal submission strategy

Possible outcomes

A completed research project, manuscript, poster, preprint, conference submission or journal submission, depending on the project and its readiness.

03Selective Programs

Build stronger applications to selective research programs.

Develop the research story, written materials and interview skills needed to present your work competitively.

You’ll work on

  • Personalized program shortlist
  • Application strategy and timeline
  • Research-interest statements
  • Essay development and revision
  • Résumé and activity descriptions
  • Recommendation strategy
  • Application review
  • Mock interviews

Programs students may target

RSI · SSP · MITES · Simons · Garcia · Clark Scholars · COSMOS · PROMYS · Ross · other selective programs

Program recommendations depend on the student’s eligibility, interests and timeline.

How the paths connect

The paths are designed to compound.

  1. Research projectQuestion, design and data
  2. CompetitionFairs, JSHS, Regeneron STS
  3. PublicationManuscript, preprint or journal
  4. Selective programsRSI, SSP, MITES and more
  5. Stronger research portfolioWork you can show

A student might spend one semester developing a project, submit it to a competition, turn the work into a manuscript, and use that experience when applying to selective summer programs. We build the roadmap around the student’s starting point rather than forcing everyone through the same sequence.

Questions families ask.

Who is it for?

High school students in grades 9–12 who want to do real research. Foundations starts at the beginning, with choosing a question.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the path, or the mix of paths. We walk through options and exact pricing on the strategy call, before you commit to anything. Monthly installments cost nothing extra. Full refund before the second session, and the unused portion refunded after that.

How many hours a week?

3–5 hours a week.

Is it online?

Yes, fully online, from the Foundations course to the weekly sessions.

Can you guarantee a professor or lab placement?

No. Whether a professor takes on a student depends on their interest and capacity. We help students find relevant labs and write strong, personalized outreach.

Do you guarantee awards, publication or admission?

No. Judges, editors and admissions offices make those decisions, not us.

Will you write any of it?

Never. Mentors ask questions and give feedback. Every word and every line of code is the student’s.

Do you help with college applications?

Only with presenting the research: the activities list, a resume and LinkedIn.
