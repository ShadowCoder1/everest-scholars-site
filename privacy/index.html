Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This page explains what information Everest Futures collects, why, and what we do with it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

What we collect

  • When you apply or ask for a strategy call: the details you type into the application form, such as names, email, grade, school and goals. The form is hosted by Airtable.
  • When you request our free guide through an ad: your name, email and your student's grade, collected by the ad platform's form (for example Meta) and passed to us.
  • When you use the student portal: account details (name and email) and the work you add, such as sessions, tasks, milestones, questions and links. The portal runs on Google Firebase. Student files stay in Google Drive folders shared with the family.
  • When you visit the site: if we turn on analytics, standard usage data such as pages viewed and the referring site. We do not use it to identify you personally.

How we use it

To reply to you, run strategy calls, deliver mentorship, send progress updates to parents, and send occasional emails about our programs. Every email has a way to opt out, and we honor it.

What we never do

  • We never sell your information.
  • We never share a student's work, results or name publicly without written permission from the student and, for anyone under 18, a parent.
  • We never contact students through social media direct messages.

Children

Our programs are for high school students. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you or your student at any time. Email hello@everestscholars.org and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top.
