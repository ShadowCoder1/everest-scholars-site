How it works.

  1. Book a free 30-minute call. We talk through the paper, the journal and any questions. A parent or guardian joins.
  2. Pay $120 and submit the finished paper. A parent or guardian makes the purchase. The 24 hours start when the paper is submitted.
  3. Professors and subject experts review it. Every paper is peer-reviewed before it is published.
  4. It’s published in the Everest Scholars Journal within 24 hours of submission. If it isn’t, we refund the full $120.

The terms, in plain words.

A finished paper is a complete research paper the student wrote, ready to publish as it is. An outline or a draft doesn’t count yet. If you aren’t sure the paper is ready, ask on the free call.

The 24 hours start when the complete paper is submitted, not when you pay or book a call. They include nights, weekends and holidays.

The refund. If the paper isn’t published within 24 hours of submission, we refund the full $120 to the card you paid with within one business day. Banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

The journal. The Everest Scholars Journal is owned and run by Everest Futures LLC, the company that sells this service. It is our in-house journal, so students list the paper as published in the Everest Scholars Journal.

Questions.

Who runs the journal?

Everest Futures LLC. The Everest Scholars Journal is our in-house journal, not an outside one.

Who reviews the papers?

Professors and subject experts. Every paper is peer-reviewed before it is published.

Do you write or edit the paper?

Never. The paper is the student’s own finished work, and every word is the student’s.

Will publication help with admissions or competitions?

Publication doesn’t guarantee admission, awards or placement in any competition. Editors, judges and admissions offices decide those. If the student enters a competition, report this service where the forms ask: Regeneron STS asks students to disclose paid publishing, ISEF’s Form 2A asks about paid support, and JSHS asks for a statement of outside help.

Is the call really free?

Yes. It’s 30 minutes with our team, with no obligation to buy anything.
